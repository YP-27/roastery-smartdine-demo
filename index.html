Our story

Roasted in-house, served without pretense.

THE ROASTERY & CO. started as a single roaster in the back of a friend's garage. Today it's a full kitchen and cafe, but the habit hasn't changed — we roast in small batches, taste everything before it goes on the menu, and keep the room comfortable enough to stay in for a while.

Open seven days a week, from the first pour-over of the morning to the last plate at night.
